# music-os

Language for a curated guitar play-along catalogue and its learning path.

## Language

**Song**:
A musical work that can have multiple performances, recordings and guitar arrangements.
_Avoid_: Recording, video (when referring to the musical work).

**Recording**:
A specific performance and edit of a song, with its own playback timeline. Different uploads may contain different edits of the same performance.
_Avoid_: Song (when identifying the version being played).

**Arrangement**:
A playable version of a song for a stated guitar setup, including chosen chord shapes and rhythm. A simpler arrangement can differ from the recorded guitar part.
_Avoid_: Transcription (unless the recorded part has actually been transcribed).

**Chord shape**:
The fingering a guitarist plays. Its sounding chord can differ when a capo or another tuning is used.
_Avoid_: Sounding chord (when describing only the fingering).

**Practice pattern**:
A suggested strumming or picking exercise for practising a song. It can be simpler than the recorded guitar part.
_Avoid_: Recorded strumming (unless checked against the recording).

**Arrangement profile**:
An arrangement's main chord shapes, its level (1–5) on the learning path and the reasons for that level. The main shapes are the shapes a learner sees for the song and the only shapes its level counts.
_Avoid_: Core shapes, chips (when meaning the set the level is judged on); difficulty (alone, when the shapes are meant too).

**Learning path**:
An ordered set of songs and practice goals that helps a guitarist progress through musical skills.
_Avoid_: Song list (when an intended skill progression is part of the meaning).

**Catalogue**:
The deliberately selected songs and recording-specific arrangements offered for practice.
_Avoid_: Any-video search (when describing the curated selection).
